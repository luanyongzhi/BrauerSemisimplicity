History and adaptations:
* verify_h3.py: Round9 (preserved in Round17); added 30 exact contravariance
  identities and a callable entrypoint. The two five-edge final-letter
  corrections are documented in the source.
* f4_model.py: roots_data, pmat, signed_rep, make_cells, verify_relations
  from Round15 audit_integer.py. Unused character/block/radical computations,
  paths and python-flint import were removed. Root transport is unchanged.
* verify_f4.py and verify_g2.py: November 14 consolidation checks; removed
  run metadata and file writes. The F4 determinant normalization includes
  the coefficient metric det([[2,1],[1,2]])=3.
* verify_h4_relations.py and verify_h4_spectrum.py: Round13 independent
  E8 reconstruction, rerun November 14. Removed saved arrays and graph/radical
  calculations not needed for the paper. Added an explicit exact polynomial
  check for the maximal determinant. The runner reuses one reconstructed
  geometry in the relation and spectral verification.
